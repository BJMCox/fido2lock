Next steps

  1. Open fido2lock from Applications. A key icon appears in the menu bar. If macOS refuses to open the app, click Done, then click "Open Anyway" in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  2. Choose "Set Up…" in the menu. Enter a label for your key and its PIN, then touch the key.
  3. In the last panel, turn on "Start at Login".

The README at github.com/BJMCox/fido2lock covers backup keys, pausing, and the terminal commands.
