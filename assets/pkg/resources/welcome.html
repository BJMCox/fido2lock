Lock your Mac when you remove your security key

fido2lock watches for your enrolled FIDO2 security keys. When you remove one, it locks the screen, at once or after a delay you set. Unlock as usual, with your password or Touch ID. Without the key, your Mac works as before.

It works with any FIDO2 key, for example YubiKey, Token2, or Google Titan.

This package installs /Applications/fido2lock.app and nothing else. It runs no scripts and writes no settings. It needs macOS 13 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
